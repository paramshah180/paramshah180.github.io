Hey! My journey began as a mechanical engineering student at University of California - San Diego. It was here that I become interested in power and energy, where I joined research group <a href="https://derconnect.ucsd.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" style="color: #8b5a2b; text-decoration: underline;">DERConnect</a> to get experience seeing what the future of power systems held. Additionally, I did class projects and internships that introduced and immersed me in the world of electronics, automation, and control systems. 

These experiences inspired my next move, a master's degree at UT Austin in electrical engineering with a concentration in power electronics and power systems. As electrical engineering wasn't my direct background, I dove headfirst into every hands on opportunity I could find: <a href="https://sites.utexas.edu/hanson/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" style="color: #8b5a2b; text-decoration: underline;">Power Electronics and Magnetics Group (PEMG)</a>, class projects, and did personal projects. Anything where I could work with hardware. 


When I'm not working on my next project, you can find me playing whatever new sport has recently caught my attention or spending time with friends and family.
